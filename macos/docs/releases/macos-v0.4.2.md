A paper's ranking pills beside the breadcrumb keep their size again.

**Install or update** (macOS 14 or newer; Zotero 10 with the bridge plugin). From 0.3.1 on, the menu bar offers it
(*Update to 0.4.2…*); otherwise:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://github.com/mbradaschia/zotero-companion-launcher/releases/download/macos-v0.4.2/install.sh | bash
```

Updates keep your configuration: they replace the app and the runner's own folder, never your settings, prompts,
taxonomies, saved searches, API keys or hotkey.

**The ranking pills aren't squeezed.** In 0.4.1 the breadcrumb took the bar's width first, so in a paper's menu the
ranks and the count beside it were squeezed. They keep their size now, and the breadcrumb wraps in the space left.
