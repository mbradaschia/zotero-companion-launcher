A wider PDF and text size buttons in the reader.

**Install or update** (macOS 14 or newer; Zotero 10 with the bridge plugin). From 0.3.1 on, the menu bar offers it
(*Update to 0.4.38…*); otherwise:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://github.com/mbradaschia/zotero-companion-launcher/releases/download/macos-v0.4.38/install.sh | bash
```

Updates keep your configuration: they replace the app and the runner's own folder, never your settings, prompts,
taxonomies, saved searches, API keys or hotkey. The bridge (0.2.7) and the prompt runner (0.3.1) are unchanged.

**The PDF pane widens as far as you drag it.** It no longer stops at 760 px: it goes on until the paper's text keeps
its 420 px, and narrows back with the window. The margin folds away to make room.

**Text size buttons.** − and + in the reader's toolbar make the text smaller and larger, a step at a time, as ⌘- and
⌘+ do (⌘0 its size).
