An icon of its own, a place in the Dock and ⌘Tab, themes you see as you browse them, and review tables that keep
each list's layout, write AI columns for you and show a long cell whole.

**Install or update** (macOS 14 or newer; Zotero 10 with the bridge plugin). From 0.3.1 on, the menu bar offers it
(*Update to 0.4.15…*); otherwise:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://github.com/mbradaschia/zotero-companion-launcher/releases/download/macos-v0.4.15/install.sh | bash
```

Updates keep your configuration: they replace the app and the runner's own folder, never your settings, prompts,
taxonomies, saved searches, API keys or hotkey. *Write it with AI* needs the runner this app brings (0.2.4):
*Settings › Setup › Install the AI features* offers it.

**An icon of its own**, in the Dock, ⌘Tab and Finder: a shelf of books and a magnifier.

**In the Dock and ⌘Tab.** A click on its Dock icon, or switching to it with nothing of it on screen, opens the
launcher as the hotkey does. Settings › General › *In the Dock and ⌘Tab* turns it off (the menu bar's icon and the
hotkey only, as before). It's kept on this Mac.

**Themes preview as you browse.** On Settings › General › Theme (and Light or dark), the row you're on is the app's
look at once. ↵ keeps it; esc or ⌫, or closing the launcher, puts back the one you had.

**Each list keeps its review table.** ⌘T on a collection, a tag, a saved search or a query that already has a table
opens that table, with its columns, their widths and order, its sort and grouping as you left them. Before, every ⌘T
made a new table with the default columns.

**+ Column asks who fills it in first**: you (text, or one of a list) or the AI, then only that kind's fields, with
*‹ Back*. The *Existing attributes* section is gone: the Columns button now lists every column there is (Zotero's
fields, the rankings, your taxonomies, your columns and the AI ones), grouped, to tick.

**Write it with AI.** In an AI column's dialog, describe what you want to know, and your prompts model writes the
column: its name, the prompt, what it reads, the answer's shape and choices (for *Jev, relevance*, the topic and
what relevant means). Change anything before *Add*; nothing is saved until then.

**The selected cell shows its whole text** when it doesn't fit, over the cells below. A click there selects the cell
under it; a double-click edits it, or opens the paper on the title.

**⌘- and ⌘+ size the table's text** (⌃- and ⌃+ too), ⌘0 puts it back. One size for every review table, kept.

**Scrolling stops where it should**: a cell, the panel or a menu scrolled to its end no longer scrolls the table on.
The toolbar's menus close on their button again, or a click outside.
