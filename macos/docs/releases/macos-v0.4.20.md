See at a glance which papers have a PDF: a paper's icon is solid with one and hollow without.

**Install or update** (macOS 14 or newer; Zotero 10 with the bridge plugin). From 0.3.1 on, the menu bar offers it
(*Update to 0.4.20…*); otherwise:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://github.com/mbradaschia/zotero-companion-launcher/releases/download/macos-v0.4.20/install.sh | bash
```

Updates keep your configuration: they replace the app and the runner's own folder, never your settings, prompts,
taxonomies, saved searches, API keys or hotkey. The runner (0.2.5) and the bridge (0.2.6) are unchanged.

**You see which papers have a PDF.** A paper's icon in the results, a collection, a tag or a saved search is solid
when it has a PDF attached and hollow when it hasn't, in its type's shape (a page for an article, a closed book for a
book, a cap for a thesis). Shape tells them apart, never colour alone. Hovering the icon says *Has a PDF* or *No
PDF*, and VoiceOver reads the same.
